GIVouch Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

The short version

  • GIVouch is free and run by Puente Digital.
  • If you write a review, we keep your email, the display name you choose (if any), your answers, and the date you confirmed you're a veteran, service member or military family member. That's it.
  • We never ask for your DD-214, Social Security number, service number, ID, or medical records.
  • Your email is never shown publicly and never sold.
  • We don't use analytics, tracking or advertising cookies.
  • You can ask us to delete your reviews and your information at any time.

Who we are

GIVouch is operated by Puente Digital, United States. Contact: puente.digital@outlook.com. GIVouch is not affiliated with the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs or any government agency.

What we collect

If you only browse: nothing that identifies you. Your language, light/dark mode and cookie choices are saved in your own browser. Our hosting provider keeps standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) for a limited time, for security and to keep the site running.

If you write a review:

InformationWhyShown publicly?
Email addressTo prevent duplicate reviews and to contact you about your review if neededNever
Display name (optional)To show who wrote the reviewYes, or "Verified veteran" if left blank
Your confirmation that you're a veteran, service member or military family member, and its dateTo support the "Verified veteran" badgeOnly the badge
Your review answers and commentThey are the reviewYes, after moderation

To block spam, the review form uses Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks technical signals from your browser (such as your IP address) to tell people from bots. Cloudflare handles that information under its own privacy policy.

What we never collect

DD-214s or other service records, Social Security numbers, service numbers, government IDs, medical records, financial account details, or your home address. Please don't include any of these in a review comment.

How we use your information

Only to run GIVouch: publishing and moderating reviews, preventing spam, duplicates and abuse, and answering your requests. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not build profiles of you.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your information, and schools never receive reviewer emails. We share information only:

  • with service providers who run the site for us: hosting (Vercel), bot protection (Cloudflare) and database (Neon);
  • when the law requires it, such as a valid court order; or
  • if GIVouch is transferred to a new owner, in which case this policy continues to apply to information already collected.

Cookies and browser storage

We use only what the site needs to work: remembering your language, theme and cookie choice, and Cloudflare's security check on the review form. No analytics, tracking or advertising cookies. See the Cookie Policy for details.

How long we keep it

  • Published reviews: until you ask us to delete them, or we remove them for breaking our review rules.
  • Your email and confirmation: as long as your review is on the site, then deleted within 90 days.
  • Rejected reviews: deleted within 12 months.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete your information and reviews by emailing puente.digital@outlook.com from the address you used. We respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example, California or the European Union), you may have additional rights, and we honor them for all users.

Children

GIVouch is for veterans, service members and their families. It is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Security

The site uses HTTPS everywhere, access to reviewer information is limited to the site administrator, and we collect as little as possible so there is little to lose. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your information, we will tell you as the law requires.

Users outside the United States

GIVouch is operated from the United States, and information is stored on U.S. servers. If you use GIVouch from abroad, your information is transferred to the United States.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date and, for important changes, post a notice on the site before the change takes effect.
