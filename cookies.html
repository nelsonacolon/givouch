GIVouch Cookie Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. GIVouch mostly uses similar browser storage instead, which works the same way, and this policy covers both.

What GIVouch uses

Only what is needed for the site to work:

NameWhat it doesHow long
LanguageRemembers English or SpanishUntil you clear your browser data
ThemeRemembers light or dark modeUntil you clear your browser data
Cookie choiceRemembers your Accept All or Reject All answerUntil you clear your browser data
Security checkCloudflare Turnstile, on the review form only, to block spam botsShort-lived, set by Cloudflare

What GIVouch does not use

No analytics, tracking, advertising or social media cookies, and no third-party trackers.

Your choices

The cookie banner offers Accept All and Reject All. Because GIVouch only uses what the site needs, both choices currently work the same way, and the site stays fully usable either way. If we ever add optional cookies, they will stay off unless you choose Accept All, and we will update this policy first.

You can change your answer at any time from the "Cookie preferences" link in the footer, or clear your browser data in your browser settings.

Tus opciones

El aviso de cookies ofrece Aceptar todo y Rechazar todo. Como GIVouch solo usa lo que el sitio necesita, ambas opciones funcionan igual por ahora, y el sitio sigue funcionando por completo con cualquiera de ellas. Si algún día agregamos cookies opcionales, permanecerán desactivadas a menos que elijas Aceptar todo, y actualizaremos esta política antes.

Puedes cambiar tu respuesta en cualquier momento desde el enlace "Preferencias de cookies" al pie de la página, o borrar los datos de tu navegador desde su configuración.
